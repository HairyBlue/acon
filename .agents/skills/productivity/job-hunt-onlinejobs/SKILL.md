# OnlineJobs.ph & Remote Job Scout + Proposal Drafter

## Use this when

The user asks to look for job postings on OnlineJobs.ph (or Upwork, LinkedIn, WeWorkRemotely, etc.), evaluate a job description, or write a tailored cover letter/application.

## Process

1. **Confirm Target Criteria:** Match listings against the user's target roles (Full-Stack, Backend, Frontend, AI/Automation, DevOps), tech stack, minimum hourly/monthly rate (USD/PHP), weekly hours (part-time, full-time, gig), and timezone overlap.
2. **Job Evaluation & Fit Table:** Extract Job Title, Employer/Company name, Offered Rate & Hours, Required Stack, Match Score (`Strong Match` / `Worth Considering` / `Skip`), and Source URL.
3. **Hidden Attention-Check Detector:** Scan the full listing text for secret screening instructions buried by employers (e.g., *"Put the word MANGO in your subject line"* or *"Answer these 3 questions in the first line"*) and call them out at the top.
4. **Red-Flag & Scam Filter:** Flag lowball rates for 5-in-1 roles, unpaid "trial projects" that build real production features, requests to move immediately to Telegram/WhatsApp, or invasive screen-monitoring demands.
5. **Anti-AI-Slop Proposal Drafter:** Write a short, direct, human-sounding OnlineJobs.ph subject line and message that obeys any attention-check keyword first, maps 2–3 concrete past outcomes to their stack, asks one smart technical/workflow question, and avoids generic AI openings.

## Output

Use: Job Match Table, Hidden Attention-Check Alert, Red-Flag Assessment, and Copy-Ready Subject Line + Proposal Draft.

## Guardrails

Never bypass logins, CAPTCHAs, or anti-bot gates, and never submit a job application automatically without explicit user action.
