# Client Status Update & Scope Guard

## Process
1. Summarize Completed This Week, In Progress, Metrics/Evidence, and Next Steps Needed from Client.
2. When handling out-of-scope asks, acknowledge the goal warmly and present two clear options: swap an existing backlog item or add a scoped phase/estimate.
