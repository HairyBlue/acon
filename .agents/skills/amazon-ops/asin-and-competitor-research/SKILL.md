# ASIN, Competitor & Margin Research

## Process
1. Compare target and competitor ASINs across price, BSR/estimated velocity, review count/rating, main image hook, and top complaints in 1-to-3-star reviews.
2. Calculate net margin after COGS, referral fee, FBA fulfillment fee, storage, and target TACOS.
3. Recommend `Go`, `Validate Further`, or `Pass` with evidence.
