# PPC Wasted-Spend & Search-Term Audit

## Process
1. Inspect campaign and search-term performance against target ACOS and break-even margin.
2. Produce three tables: (1) Negative Keyword Candidates (high clicks/spend, 0 orders), (2) Graduation Candidates (converting auto/broad terms ready for exact match), and (3) Bid/Budget Adjustments.
